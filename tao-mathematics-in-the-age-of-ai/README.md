# Mathematics in the Age of AI

- **Full title:** Mathematics in the Age of AI
- **Author:** Terence Tao
- **Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16753 (PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.16753)
- **License:** arXiv.org perpetual non-exclusive license

## About

Essay based on a public lecture at the 2026 International Congress of Mathematicians. Conditions on the arrival of AI tools capable of research-level mathematical tasks and asks what the goals and values of mathematical research actually are, using problem-solving as a case study. Touches formalization, peer review, Goodhart's law, and mathematical values.
