# Helpful, harmless, honest? Sociotechnical limits of AI alignment and safety through Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

- **Full title:** Helpful, harmless, honest? Sociotechnical limits of AI alignment and safety through Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback
- **Authors:** Adam Dahlgren Lindström, Leila Methnani, Lea Krause, Petter Ericson, Íñigo Martínez de Rituerto de Troya, Dimitri Coelho Mollo, Roel Dobbe
- **Date:** 2025 (Ethics Inf Technol 27:28)
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-025-09837-2
- **PMC:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12137480/

## About

Multidisciplinary critique of RLHF/RLAIF as a means of value alignment and AI safety. Argues the HHH principle (helpful, harmless, honest) has deep theoretical and practical limits; situates RLHF within a broader sociotechnical design of institutions, processes, and systems rather than as a silver bullet.
